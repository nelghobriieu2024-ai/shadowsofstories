# SHADOWS

A photographic series by Nour El Ghobri about perfectionism, always needing something to look forward to, and the pressure to make everything mean something.

I never appear in the frame. Only my shadow does. The series follows six moments of one cycle, and the website lets you walk around that cycle like a small exhibition.

**Main question:** Can I keep moving forward without skipping the moment I just finished?

Live site: https://nelghobriieu2024-ai.github.io/shadowsofstories/

## The six moments

1. Something to look forward to
2. Nothing planned
3. Something new begins
4. The pressure to perform
5. Comparing myself (black and white)
6. Moving on

After moment 6 the cycle returns to moment 1.

## How to use the site

- Click the numbers on the ring in the top right corner to open a moment.
- Use the two small arrows, the arrow keys, the mouse wheel, or swipe on a phone to move to the next or previous screen.
- Keys 1 to 6 jump straight to a moment.

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole website (HTML, CSS and JavaScript in one file)
- `01.jpg` to `06.jpg`: the six photographs

No build step and no dependencies. Open `index.html` in a browser, or publish the repository with GitHub Pages.
